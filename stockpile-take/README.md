# Stockpile Take

Taking things out of a stockpile is one shift-click per item. This addon puts an amount box and a **Take**
button under the pile in every stockpile window, so you say how many you want and get them in one press.

## Use

Open a stockpile the way you always do — right-click the pile — and the window has one extra row under it:

| You do | It does |
|---|---|
| type a number and press **Take** | takes that many items out of the pile |
| type a number and press **Enter** | the same, without reaching for the button |
| press **Take** with the field as it is | takes the one item the field starts on |

The whole amount leaves the pile at once, and lands wherever a shift-click on the pile would have put it.

## Notes

- Ask for more than the pile holds and you get what is there.
- One press takes at most 300 items, which is more than a pile holds.
- The row is added to every stockpile window, on every character you have logged in.

## Permissions

- `widget.send` — takes from the pile for you, with the message your own shift-click sends.
