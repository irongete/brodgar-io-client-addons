# Crop Stage Indicator

Writes the growth stage over every crop in view, on the ground where it stands, so a field says at a
glance which parts of it are ready.

## Usage

1. Assign a key in **Options ▸ Game ▸ Keybindings ▸ Crop Stage Indicator**. Addon hotkeys start unbound,
   so nothing is claimed until you assign it.
2. Press it to show the numbers, and again to take them away.

The number is the stage the crop is on, counted from 1. How many stages a crop has before it can be
harvested depends on the crop.

## Notes

- A crop that comes into view, or advances a stage, is picked up while the numbers are on.
- Trellises are left alone: they are the frame a vine grows on, not a crop of their own.
- The numbers are drawn in white with a dark outline, so they read over pale soil and dark leaves alike.
